IPHONE PREPARATION GUIDE

How to Prepare Your Device For Trade-In

A clear, step-by-step reference sheet to prepare your device for trade-in, sale, repair, or factory reset

Important Security Notice: Disabling these features removes Activation Lock and Location Tracking, leaving your device unprotected if lost or stolen. Only perform these steps if you are selling, trading in, giving away, or sending your iPhone for service.
1

Turn Off Stolen Device Protection (iOS 17.3 or later)

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Scroll down and tap Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode on older models). Enter your passcode when prompted.
  3. Scroll down to Stolen Device Protection and tap to manage settings.
  4. Tap the switch to turn Stolen Device Protection OFF (the switch will turn grey).
Mandatory Security Delay Note: If you are away from a designated "Familiar Location" (such as your home or workplace), iOS will enforce a mandatory 1-hour security delay before this setting fully turns off. Once the delay elapses, return to this screen to complete deactivation.
2

Turn Off Find My iPhone & Activation Lock

  1. Return to the main page of the Settings app.
  2. Tap your Apple ID / Name banner at the very top of the settings menu.
  3. Tap Find My, then select Find My iPhone at the top of the screen.
  4. Tap the green toggle switch next to Find My iPhone to turn it off.
  5. When prompted, enter your Apple ID Password and tap Turn Off in the upper right corner. The toggle switch will turn grey to confirm it is disabled.

💬 WhatsApp Backup Guide (iPhone / iOS)

Before You Start: Connect to Wi-Fi, ensure Cloud Account Signed In, check Free Storage Available, and Battery > 50%.

  1. Open iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Turn WhatsApp ON.
  2. Open WhatsApp, tap Settings tab at bottom.
  3. Navigate to Chats > Chat Backup.
  4. Tap Auto Backup to select daily or weekly schedule.
  5. Tap Back Up Now to execute a manual cloud backup.
Requirement: Available iCloud space must be at least 2.05x the size of your backup.

ANDROID PREPARATION GUIDE

WhatsApp Backup Step-by-Step Reference

A clear, step-by-step reference sheet to back up your WhatsApp data before trade-in

1

WhatsApp Backup (Google Drive)

Before You Start: Connect to Wi-Fi, ensure Cloud Account Signed In, check Free Storage Available, and Battery > 50%.

  1. Open WhatsApp and tap More options (three dots) at top right.
  2. Tap Settings > Chats > Chat backup.
  3. Select Google Account to link your storage account.
  4. Set Back up to Google Drive frequency (Daily/Weekly).
  5. Tap the green BACK UP button to start immediate backup.
Tip: Turn on "Include videos" only if you have sufficient Google Drive storage.
